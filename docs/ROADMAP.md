"Needs" lists the [backlog](BACKLOG.md) gaps the owner has to settle before that version can be built without guessing. The first run's pacing target (about 3 hours to £1M) and its checkpoints are in [ECONOMY.md](ECONOMY.md#the-first-run-g5-d005).
